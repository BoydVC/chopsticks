CHOPSTICKS

Fly the booster home. Let the tower catch it.

Controls

Space light engines · W/S or ↑/↓ throttle · Shift full · X cut
A/D or ←/→ steer (the flight computer works the gimbal and grid fins)
C camera · V auto-director · R replay/retry · T autopilot (unranked) · P pause · M mute · Esc menu
Touch: steer pads on the left, throttle slider on the right.

How to catch

Glide on the grid fins toward the tower, light the engines late (fuel is limited) and settle the catch pins onto the arms: slow, upright and centred. Super Heavy has no legs, so the ground is not an option.
